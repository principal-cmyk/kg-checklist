How to read the scores. Each domain's raw score converts to a scaled score from 1 to 19 using the student's age band (A: 3y1m–4y0m, B: 4y1m–5y0m, C: 5y1m–5y11m): 1–3 significant delay · 4–6 slight delay · 7–13 average · 14–16 slightly advanced · 17–19 highly advanced. The seven scaled scores add up to a Development Index (mean 100): 69 or below significant delay · 70–79 slight delay · 80–119 average · 120–129 slightly advanced · 130 or above highly advanced. Scores are counted only when every item of an assessment has been marked.
